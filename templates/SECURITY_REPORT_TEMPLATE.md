# Private vulnerability report

Submit this content only through the affected repository's private vulnerability reporting channel or another private channel approved by its maintainers. Do not open a public issue with vulnerability details.

## Summary

Describe the suspected vulnerability and affected service.

## Affected versions and commits

List known affected releases, commit hashes, and deployment context.

## Impact and prerequisites

Describe what an attacker could do and what access or conditions are required.

## Reproduction

Provide minimal, safe steps. Do not include live credentials, customer data, or destructive payloads.

## Evidence

Attach sanitized logs, screenshots, or a proof of concept that avoids accessing other users' data.

## Suggested mitigation

Optional containment or remediation ideas.

## Reporter coordination

Preferred private contact and any disclosure timing constraints.
