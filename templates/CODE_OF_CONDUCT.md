# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good faith, give and receive actionable feedback, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use public issue trackers for sensitive reports.

This policy applies in organization repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, and other project spaces.
